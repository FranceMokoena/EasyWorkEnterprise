ABOUT EASYWORK ENTERPRISE

Reliable material supply and delivery for businesses and organisations.

Easywork Enterprise (Pty) Ltd is a materials supply and delivery business focused on practical, dependable supply solutions. The company specialises in plastic bags, heavy-duty plastic bags, refuse bags and other plastic products, alongside general material supply and delivery support.

The business supports customers and organisations requiring consistent supply across Mpumalanga and South Africa, with delivery arrangements built around the practical needs of the customer.

Core focus

  • Heavy-duty plastic bags
  • Refuse bags
  • Other plastic products
  • General material supply
  • Reliable delivery
  • School stationery
